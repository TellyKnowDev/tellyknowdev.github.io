TellyKnow privacy statement

Effective October 27, 2026

TellyKnow is made for children, so I keep data to a minimum. In short: no ads from TellyKnow, no tracking, no selling data, and nothing that identifies your child.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your grown-up settings: age bracket, blocked words, blocked channels, the screen-time timer and Time's up!.
  • Kids' profiles, if you add them: a nickname and a picture for each kid, used only to greet them and keep their settings.
  • Your grown-up passcode, stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Minutes watched per channel, shown in Watch summary.
  • A list of videos that couldn't play, so they're hidden next time.

Deleting TellyKnow deletes all of this.

Anonymous watch time we collect

  • On devices signed in to iCloud, TellyKnow shares how many minutes each channel was watched: totals for each of the last 30 days, plus an all-time total.
  • It's sent at most once an hour with a random ID made by the app, so each device updates its own totals. That ID isn't linked to your child, your name, your email or your Apple Account.
  • It's stored in Apple's iCloud. iCloud attaches an anonymous, app-specific account code to it, which doesn't reveal who you are.
  • I use it only to see which channels families watch, so I can decide what to add or remove. It's never used for advertising or profiling, and never sold or shared.
  • Sharing is part of how TellyKnow works and can't be turned off. Devices not signed in to iCloud don't share anything.
  • Reset watch summary (in Watch summary) also clears that device's shared totals the next time it connects.

YouTube

  • Videos play through YouTube's official embedded player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. TellyKnow also loads channel lists and video thumbnails from YouTube.
  • When this happens, Google receives the information it normally gets from a web request, such as your IP address, and may show ads in videos. This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • TellyKnow doesn't sign in to YouTube and doesn't share any of your information with Google.

Purchases

  • TellyKnow Star and tips are bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; I never see your name, email or card details.
  • The app only checks with Apple whether Star is active on your Apple Account.

Email

If you email tellyknow@icloud.com, I'll see your email address and message. I use them only to reply and to review channel requests, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

What TellyKnow doesn't do

  • No accounts, names, photos, locations or contacts.
  • No ads from TellyKnow, no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Children's privacy

TellyKnow is designed for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. The only information shared is the anonymous watch time described above, used only to run and improve the app. Grown-up features, purchases and links sit behind a grown-up check.

Changes and contact

If this statement changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions? Email tellyknow@icloud.com.
