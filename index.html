Quick help

  • Forgot your passcode? On the passcode screen, tap Forgot passcode? and use Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.
  • Manage or cancel TellyKnow Star: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
